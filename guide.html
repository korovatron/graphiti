Overview

What is Graphiti?

Graphiti is an online graphing calculator, graph plotter and curve exploration tool. It is designed for students, teachers and anyone who wants to understand the behaviour of mathematical graphs rather than only draw them.

It runs in the browser, works on desktop and mobile, and can be installed as a Progressive Web App for offline use.

What makes Graphiti different from a basic function plotter?

A basic function plotter draws curves. Graphiti also tries to explain what is happening on the graph by detecting and displaying mathematical features such as turning points, intersections, intercepts, asymptotes, holes, envelopes and recognisable curve types.

Graphiti also includes interactive tracing, tangent and normal lines, integral regions, polar plotting and editable SVG export for worksheets and slides.

Plotting tools

What types of graphs can Graphiti plot?

Graphiti can plot several types of mathematical graphs:

  • Explicit functions such as y = x2, y = sin(x) and y = 1 / x.
  • Implicit equations such as x2 + y2 = 25.
  • Explicit and implicit inequalities with shaded regions.
  • Polar functions such as r = 1 + cos(θ).
  • Parametric curves such as (cos(t), sin(t)).
Can Graphiti plot implicit equations?

Yes. Graphiti can act as an implicit equation plotter for relations involving both x and y. This is useful for circles, ellipses, curves with vertical parts, relations that are not functions of x alone, and equations where solving explicitly for y would be awkward.

Can Graphiti plot inequalities?

Yes. Graphiti supports inequality graphing, including shaded regions, strict and non-strict boundaries, and intersections between multiple inequalities.

Can Graphiti plot polar and parametric graphs?

Yes. Graphiti can plot polar functions, polar rays and parametric curves. Polar mode includes controls for θ ranges, optional negative r plotting, and animation controls for stepping through how a polar curve is drawn.

This makes polar graphs easier to demonstrate in class, because students can see the curve build up as θ changes rather than only seeing the finished shape.

Graph analysis

What graph features can Graphiti detect automatically?

Graphiti can automatically identify several important graph features, including intersections, intercepts, turning points, asymptotes, removable singularities and envelopes for suitable functions.

It can also classify many recognised curve types, including lemniscates, semi-cubical parabolas, hyperbolas and other named curves when their equations match supported forms.

These features are designed to support curve sketching, visual explanation and classroom demonstration.

Can Graphiti show tangents and normals?

Yes. You can place trace markers on curves and cycle them through tangent, normal and integral modes. Tangent and normal lines are useful when exploring gradients, differentiation and local behaviour.

Can Graphiti show area and integration?

Graphiti can shade and calculate definite integral regions and area between curves. It also includes visual numerical integration methods such as the trapezium rule, Simpson's rule and the mid-ordinate rule.

Classroom use

Is Graphiti useful for GCSE and A-Level maths?

Graphiti is designed with GCSE, A-Level and classroom explanation in mind. It can support topics such as curve sketching, transformations, differentiation, integration, inequalities, numerical methods, parametric equations and polar graphs.

It can also help students see why features such as asymptotes, stationary points, holes and intersections matter.

Can I share graphs with students?

Yes. Graphiti can create shareable URLs that restore a complete graph setup, including functions, viewport and analysis markers. This makes it easier to prepare lesson examples, demonstrations and revision tasks.

Can I export graphs for worksheets or slides?

Yes. Graphiti can export graphs as PNG or SVG. SVG export is especially useful for worksheets, slides and documents because the graph remains crisp and editable in many design and office tools.

FAQ

Is Graphiti free?

Yes. Graphiti is free to use in the browser.

Does Graphiti work on phones and tablets?

Yes. Graphiti works on desktop, tablet and mobile browsers. It includes touch controls for panning and zooming, and it can be installed as a Progressive Web App on supported devices.

Does Graphiti replace Desmos or GeoGebra?

Desmos and GeoGebra are excellent general-purpose graphing tools. Graphiti has a different emphasis: visual curve exploration, automatic graph analysis, implicit plotting, calculus annotations, SVG export and shareable classroom setups.

What specialist graphing tasks is Graphiti designed for?

Graphiti is designed for tasks that go beyond basic function plotting, including implicit equation plotting, polar graphs, inequality graphing, tangents and normals, area between curves, and A-Level curve sketching.

It is especially useful when you want a graphing calculator that can show structure as well as shape.
