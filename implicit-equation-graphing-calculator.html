Implicit equation graphing calculator

Graphiti plots implicit equations involving both x and y, so students can explore relations that are awkward or impossible to rewrite as a single explicit function.

It is especially useful for A-Level Maths and Further Maths conics. Circles, ellipses, parabolas and hyperbolas are plotted cleanly, and Graphiti can detect asymptotes where they occur.

What implicit plotting adds

Implicit equations let x and y appear together in one relation, such as \(x^2 + y^2 = 25\). That makes them ideal for graphing conic sections, curves with vertical parts, multiple branches and relations where solving for y would hide important behaviour.

  • Plot equations involving both x and y.
  • Graph standard conics including circles, ellipses, parabolas and hyperbolas.
  • Show curves with vertical sections or multiple branches.
  • Detect asymptotes for suitable implicit curves, including hyperbolas.
  • Support A-Level Maths and Further Maths curve sketching and conics work.

Why it helps

Many important curves in A-Level and Further Maths are naturally written as relations rather than explicit functions. Plotting them directly helps students see the whole shape instead of working through several rearranged branches.

For conics, this means the familiar equations produce the expected geometric shapes quickly. For hyperbolas and other curves with asymptotic behaviour, Graphiti can also help connect the plotted curve with the asymptotes that structure it.
