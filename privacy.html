Graphiti privacy policy

Effective date: 5 October 2026

Graphiti (www.korovatron.co.uk/graphiti) is a free online graphing calculator by Korovatron (Neil Kendall). It works fully without an account. Signing in with Google is optional and is only used for cloud saves, so you can open your graphs on another device. This page explains what data Graphiti collects, how it is used, shared, stored and deleted. It sits alongside the Korovatron privacy policy, which covers the rest of the site.

Who is responsible for your data

The data controller is Korovatron (Neil Kendall). For any privacy question or request, email nimatrix@korovatron.co.uk.

If you do not sign in

  • Your functions, graph settings and preferences are stored only in your browser on your device (local storage). They are not sent to us.
  • Share links and QR codes contain your graph inside the part of the web address after the #. Browsers do not send that part to the server, so we do not receive it. Anyone you give the link to can open the graph.
  • The site uses privacy-focused, cookie-free aggregate analytics (GoatCounter) to count visits, as described in the Korovatron privacy policy.

If you sign in with Google for cloud saves

Google user data Graphiti accesses

  • Graphiti uses Google Sign-In (through Firebase Authentication) with only the basic sign-in scopes: openid, email and profile. It does not request access to your Gmail, Google Drive, Contacts, Calendar or any other Google service.
  • Through those scopes Graphiti receives your Google account ID, email address, display name and profile picture link.

What we store

  • Your Google account ID, email address, name and profile picture link, held by Firebase Authentication so that you stay signed in. Google handles your password, and we never see it.
  • The graphs you choose to save: the name you give each one, its mode (Cartesian or polar), its functions and view settings, and when it was last saved.

What we use it for

  • Only to sign you in and to save, list, open, rename and delete your own graphs. Your saved graphs can only be read or changed by your own account.
  • Your email address and name are used only to identify your account and to show you that you are signed in. We do not email you.
  • We do not sell your data, use it for advertising, profile you, or share it with anyone else. We do not use Firebase or Google Analytics to track you.
  • Graphiti's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. We do not use Google user data for advertising, we do not transfer it to others except as needed to provide cloud saves or to comply with the law, and no humans read it except where you ask us for support, where needed for security, or where the law requires.

Who it is shared with

  • Only Google, as our infrastructure provider (Firebase Authentication and Cloud Firestore). We do not share it with any other third party.

Where it is kept

  • Sign-in and saved graphs are provided by Google Firebase (Firebase Authentication and Cloud Firestore), with Google acting as our processor. Google may process data in the UK, the EU or other countries under its own safeguards and terms.
  • Your browser also keeps a few small items on your device so the app can remember you are signed in and which cloud graph you last saved or opened. Signing out clears the link to your saved graphs.

Legal basis (UK GDPR)

  • We process this data to provide the cloud saves you ask for.

Keeping and deleting your data

  • Saved graphs stay in your account until you delete them using the bin icon in the Cloud dialog. Deleting a graph removes it from our database.
  • Your account details are kept for as long as you keep using cloud saves. To have your account and everything stored with it deleted, email nimatrix@korovatron.co.uk from the address you signed in with, and we will delete it within 30 days.
  • You can also stop Graphiti accessing your Google account at any time at myaccount.google.com/connections.
  • Data is sent over encrypted connections (HTTPS) and stored by Google Firebase, and access rules ensure each account can only read and change its own graphs.
  • On a shared or school computer, please use Sign out in the Cloud dialog when you finish.

Pupils and schools

Graphiti is used in classrooms. Nothing in Graphiti requires an account, and teachers can use it entirely without one. Pupils should only sign in if their school or parent or carer is happy for them to use a Google account with Graphiti.

Your rights

You can ask to access, correct, delete, restrict or object to our use of your personal data, and where applicable to receive a copy of it. Email nimatrix@korovatron.co.uk to make a request. You can also complain to the UK Information Commissioner's Office at ico.org.uk.

Changes

If we change how Graphiti handles data, we will update this page and its effective date.
